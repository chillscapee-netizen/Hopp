Questions and answers

The most common questions. Can't find yours? Ask the person who invited you, or look in Discover inside the app.

Getting started

What do I need?

A computer with Windows 10 or 11. Hopp is about 5 MB.

Windows says it doesn't know Hopp

Hopp is new, so Windows hasn't seen it much yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Do I need an email address?

No. You make a Hopp ID with a name and a password. No email, no phone number.

Why do I need a recovery phrase?

Your account is a key that lives on your computer, not a password kept on a server. The 24 words are that key written out, so you can get back in if you forget your password or move to a new computer. Read more

Do I have to save the phrase as a file?

No. Writing it on paper is safest. Saving it as a file is only there if you'd rather keep it somewhere private on your computer or a USB stick. Where to keep it

Is this some crypto thing?

No. There's no crypto, no wallet and no money. Hopp uses the same kind of word list to write out your key in words people can copy by hand.

I forgot my password

Use your recovery phrase: on the login screen, choose to recover your account and type it in. Without it, nobody can get the account back, not even Hopp.

Is there a Mac or phone version?

Not yet. Hopp is for Windows for now.

Friends and servers

How do I add a friend?

Go Home, open Add friend and type their username. Once they accept, you can message and call them.

How do I make a server?

Press + in the server list and choose Create a server. Then invite people with a link or straight from your friends list.

How do I join a server?

Click an invite link someone sent you, or press + and choose Join a server.

Can I change my name in just one server?

Yes: open the server's menu and choose Change nickname.

Voice, calls and screens

People can't hear me, or I can't hear them

Open Settings, then Voice & audio, and check your microphone and headset. The mic test shows if your microphone picks you up. Also check that you aren't muted.

How many people can be in a voice channel or call?

Up to 10 at once. Voice goes straight between you, which keeps it free and private.

How do I share my screen?

Join a voice channel or start a call, then press the screen button in the panel above your name.

What is push to talk?

Your microphone only sends while you hold a key. Turn it on in Settings, then Voice & audio, and pick the key under Keybinds.

Hopp itself

What does beta mean?

Hopp works and you can use it every day, but it's still young: you'll find bugs, and things can change as it gets better. Your account and messages are kept between versions. More about the beta

Something is broken. How do I report it?

In Hopp, open Settings, then App, and press Copy next to Debug info. Send it, with what happened, to the person who invited you or to whoever runs your server.

Is it really free?

Yes, all of it. Hopp Cloud runs on a free plan, which is why it has a few limits.

What are the limits?

Channels keep their newest 1,000 messages, up to 10 people talk in a voice channel, pictures are 40 a day, and on a very busy day Hopp Cloud can pause for a few hours. Every limit, and why

How do updates work?

When a new version is ready, the arrow at the top of Hopp turns green. You choose when to install it.

Hopp Cloud seems to be down

On a very busy day the free plan's daily limit can be reached, and Hopp Cloud pauses for a few hours. Your messages and servers are safe; it comes back by itself.

Can I run my own server?

Yes. In the app, press + and choose Run your own server to see how. It has no limits beyond your own computer.
