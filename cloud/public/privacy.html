Privacy & safety

What stays private

Plain answers about what's encrypted, what Hopp Cloud keeps, and how you stay in control.

End-to-end encrypted

  • Direct messages and group conversations
  • Pictures and files between friends
  • Calls with friends, voice and video

Only the people in the conversation can read or hear them. Not even Hopp Cloud.

Encrypted on the way

  • Voice and cameras in voice channels
  • Screen sharing

They go straight between the people in the channel whenever possible. When a network blocks that, they pass through a relay that can't read them.

What Hopp Cloud keeps

To work, Hopp Cloud keeps your username, display name, profile picture, banner, About me and your friends list. Your settings and server list are kept too, sealed so only your Hopp ID can read them.

Messages in servers are kept on Hopp Cloud so everyone in the server can see them, the newest 1,000 per channel. They aren't end-to-end encrypted, the same as on other chat apps' servers. If you want them on your own computer instead, run your own server.

What it doesn't do

No ads, no tracking, no selling anything about you. Hopp doesn't need your email or phone number.

Your Hopp ID

You sign in with a key that's made and kept on your own computer, locked with your password. Your recovery phrase is the only way back in if you forget the password, so write it down somewhere safe. Hopp warns you if you're about to send it to anyone.

Staying safe

  • Decide who may send you friend requests: everyone, friends of friends, or nobody
  • Block anyone: they can't message you or reach you
  • Server moderators can time people out, use slowmode, and ban
  • Everything moderators do is in the audit log
