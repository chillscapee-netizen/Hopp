What makes it different

Built for friends, not for profit.

Your own server, with one button

Start a server on your own computer from inside Hopp. No ports to open, nothing to change on your router, and nobody sees your address. It has everything a server on Hopp Cloud has, its messages and pictures stay on your computer, and nobody can shut it down or take your friends away.

Your IP address stays hidden

Voice, calls, screen sharing and files go through a relay by default, so nobody you talk to can see your internet address. You can turn that off if you'd rather go straight to your friends.

Private where it matters

Messages, pictures and calls between friends are end-to-end encrypted. A server on your own computer is encrypted all the way, even when your friends reach it through Hopp Cloud.

Nothing to pay, nothing to prove

Everything is free. No email, no phone number, no ID or face check.

Private, not lawless

Hopp can't read what friends send each other, but anyone can report a message, a person or a server with one right-click, and every report is read by a person. Rules & reporting

Open source

The code is on GitHub, so anyone can check what Hopp does. What's encrypted, and what Hopp Cloud keeps

What it is

Hopp is a place to hang out with your friends. Make a server for your group with text channels to write in and voice channels to talk in, call each other, share your screen, and send messages that only you and your friends can read.

What you get

  • Voice channels you just click into, with cameras and screen sharing in full quality.
  • Calls with a friend or a whole group.
  • Chat with replies, reactions, GIFs, pictures, polls, voice messages, threads and search.
  • Private messages and groups between friends, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Roles, private channels and the tools to keep a server calm.
  • Themes, fonts, a banner and a status, so it looks the way you like.

Why it exists

I wanted somewhere to hang out with my friends that doesn't keep asking for money, doesn't want my ID, and doesn't slow my games down. So I'm making it. Hopp is made by one person, it's not a company and it doesn't sell anything. The longer story

Free, with a few limits

Everything in Hopp is free. Hopp Cloud runs on a free plan, so it has a few limits: up to 10 people talking in one voice channel, the newest 1,000 messages kept in each channel, and 40 pictures a day. All the limits, and why

Getting started

Download Hopp, pick a username and a password, and make a server on Hopp Cloud or on your own computer. Then send your friends an invite link. No email and no phone number. You'll get 24 words to keep somewhere safe; they're how you get back in if you forget your password. How your Hopp ID works

Good to know

Hopp is in beta: it works every day, but it's young, so expect the odd bug. It's for Windows for now. Get it from the Microsoft Store, where it's called HoppChat: Microsoft checks and signs it, so Windows trusts it. The installer on this site isn't signed yet, so Windows may warn about it.
