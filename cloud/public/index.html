What it is

Hopp is a place to hang out with your friends. Make a server for your group with text channels to write in and voice channels to talk in, call each other, share your screen, and send messages that only you and your friends can read.

What you get

  • Voice channels you just click into, with cameras and screen sharing in full quality.
  • Calls with a friend or a whole group.
  • Chat with replies, reactions, GIFs, pictures, polls, voice messages, threads and search.
  • Private messages and groups between friends, end-to-end encrypted.
  • Roles, private channels and the tools to keep a server calm.
  • Themes, fonts, a banner and a status, so it looks the way you like.

Everything Hopp can do

Why it exists

I wanted somewhere to hang out with my friends that doesn't keep asking for money, doesn't want my ID, and doesn't slow my games down. So I'm making it. Hopp is made by one person, it's not a company and it doesn't sell anything. The longer story

Free, with a few limits

Everything in Hopp is free. Hopp Cloud runs on a free plan, so it has a few limits: up to 10 people talking in one voice channel, the newest 1,000 messages kept in each channel, and 40 pictures a day. All the limits, and why

Getting started

Download Hopp, pick a username and a password, and send your friends an invite link. No email and no phone number. You'll get 24 words to keep somewhere safe; they're how you get back in if you forget your password. How your Hopp ID works

Good to know

Hopp is in beta: it works every day, but it's young, so expect the odd bug. It's for Windows for now. Hopp isn't code-signed yet, so the first time you install it Windows may say it doesn't recognise it; choose More info, then Run anyway. Help and questions
