Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in Hopp is free and there's nothing to buy. Hopp Cloud runs on a free plan, which is why it has a few limits.

Windows says it doesn't know Hopp

Hopp is new, so Windows hasn't seen it much yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

How many people fit in a voice channel?

Up to 10 at once. Voice goes straight between you, which keeps it free and private.

Why do I get 24 words?

They're the key to your account, so you can get back in if you forget your password. Hopp has no email reset, because it never holds your password. How your Hopp ID works

Is there a Mac or phone version?

Not yet. Hopp is for Windows for now.
