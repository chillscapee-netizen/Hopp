# # #

Make a server for your friends, with text channels to write in and voice channels to talk in. Turn on your camera, share your screen, or just call someone. It's free, all of it: no ads, no tracking and nothing to unlock.

Messages between friends are end-to-end encrypted, and calls go straight between you whenever they can.

Hopp Cloud runs on a free plan, so it has a few limits: channels keep their newest 1,000 messages, up to 10 people talk in a voice channel at once, and on a very busy day it can pause for a few hours. Every limit, and why it's there, is in the app under About Hopp. Or run your own server and have none.
