Hopp Cloud, honestly

Why there are a few limits

Hopp Cloud runs on a free plan. That's how Hopp stays free with no ads. A free plan only does so much each day and stores so much, so Hopp keeps things small: channels keep their newest 1,000 messages, up to 10 people talk in a voice channel at once (voice goes straight between you), and on a very busy day Hopp Cloud can pause for a few hours. Every limit and why it's there is in the app, under Discover → About Hopp. Want none? Run your own server.
