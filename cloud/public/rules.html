Rules

Rules & reporting

Hopp is private, not lawless. These are the rules for using Hopp and Hopp Cloud, how to report someone who breaks them, and what happens then. They're short on purpose.

Who can use Hopp

You need to be at least 13, or older if the law where you live says so. By using Hopp you agree to these rules.

What isn't allowed

  • Anything sexual involving a child. It is always reported to the police.
  • Sharing sexual pictures or videos of someone without their consent
  • Threats, harassment and bullying, and sharing someone's private details to hurt them
  • Hate against people for who they are
  • Promoting terror or violence, or encouraging someone to hurt themselves
  • Scams, fraud, spam, malware and stolen accounts or data
  • Selling or arranging anything illegal
  • Trying to break Hopp Cloud, get around its limits, or get into other people's accounts

Server owners can make stricter rules for their own servers, and moderators can enforce them.

Reporting

In the app, right-click a message, a person or a server and choose Report. Pick what's wrong and add anything that helps. The report goes to Hopp with what you reported, as you see it, and who you are. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

Messages between friends are end-to-end encrypted, so Hopp can't read them. A report is the only way Hopp sees one: you're choosing to show it.

If someone is in danger right now, call the emergency number (112 in Sweden and the EU) first.

What happens then

Every report is read by a person. The most serious ones (children, violence, terror) are read first. If something breaks these rules or the law, Hopp can remove it from Hopp Cloud, close a server on Hopp Cloud, or ban an account from Hopp Cloud. Reports that are clearly false, sent again and again, can get the reporter banned too.

Sexual content involving a child, and threats to someone's life or safety, are reported to the police.

If Hopp acts on something of yours and you think it was a mistake, say so on GitHub (without posting the content itself) and it will be looked at again.

Servers on your own computer

A server you run yourself is yours: its messages and pictures are on your computer, not Hopp Cloud, and you're responsible for what's on it, the same as for a website you host. Hopp can't see into it or remove things from it. What Hopp can do is stop people from reaching it through Hopp's tunnel, and stop accounts from using Hopp Cloud.

The police and the courts

Hopp answers lawful requests from the police and courts. What Hopp has is little: what's listed under What Hopp Cloud keeps, and messages in servers on Hopp Cloud. It doesn't have messages between friends (they're encrypted), anything on servers people run themselves, email addresses or phone numbers, because Hopp never asks for them.

Contact

For the police, courts and authorities (this is Hopp's contact point under the EU Digital Services Act), and for anything about these rules: chillscapee@gmail.com. English or Swedish. To report something in Hopp, use Report in the app instead: it reaches Hopp faster and shows what you saw.

Changes

If these rules change, it says so here and on What's new.
