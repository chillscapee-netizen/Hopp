Limits

Every limit, and why

Hopp is free, all of it. To stay that way, Hopp Cloud runs on a free plan, and a free plan only does so much each day and only stores so much. Here is every limit, out in the open.

A few hours
Busy days

The free plan has a daily limit for the whole of Hopp Cloud. On a very busy day it can be reached, and Hopp Cloud pauses for a few hours. Your messages and servers are safe meanwhile.

1,000
Messages kept per channel

Each channel keeps its newest 1,000 messages, and each conversation between friends its newest 5,000. Storage is what a free plan has least of.

40 a day
Pictures

Hopp makes pictures small (about 300 KB at most). You can share 40 a day in each server and 40 a day with friends, and a server keeps up to 100 MB of them.

10
People in voice

Everyone in a voice channel sends their voice and video to everyone else, not through one server. That keeps it free and private, but past 10 people a home connection can't keep up.

25
People in a group

Group conversations are encrypted for each person in them, so they stay small.

12 a day
Relay sign-ins

Voice, calls and screens go through a relay so nobody sees your IP address (and when two people can't reach each other directly). Each person gets 12 sign-ins a day; one lasts all day.

720p
Screens, when the free relay runs low

Shared screens go out in the size you choose. The relay has a free amount each month; only when most of it is used do screens go through it at 720p, until the month turns over. Turning off Hide my IP address lifts it.

1 a second
Spam protection

A burst of 5 messages, then one a second. 10 new servers and 30 friend requests a day per person. It keeps bots and spam out, not you.

What doesn't count

Talking doesn't use up Hopp Cloud's daily limit: voice, calls, cameras and screens go between you and your friends, through the relay when your IP is hidden, not through Hopp Cloud's servers.

Your own server

A server on your own computer keeps its messages and pictures there, not on Hopp Cloud, so they don't count against Hopp Cloud's storage. It works the same way as a server on Hopp Cloud, with the same rules. In the app, press + in the server list and choose Run your own server. How it stays private

Will they change?

Hopp is in beta. If a limit changes, it says so here and on What's new.
