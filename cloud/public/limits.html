Limits

Every limit, and why

Hopp is free, all of it. To stay that way, Hopp Cloud runs on a free plan, and a free plan only does so much each day and only stores so much. Here is every limit, out in the open.

A few hours
Busy days

The free plan has a daily limit for the whole of Hopp Cloud. On a very busy day it can be reached, and Hopp Cloud pauses for a few hours. Your messages and servers are safe meanwhile.

1,000
Messages kept per channel

Each channel keeps its newest 1,000 messages, and each conversation between friends its newest 5,000. Storage is what a free plan has least of.

40 a day
Pictures

Hopp makes pictures small (about 300 KB at most). You can share 40 a day in each server and 40 a day with friends, and a server keeps up to 100 MB of them.

10
People in voice

Voice and video go straight between the people talking, not through a server. That keeps calls free and private, but everyone sends to everyone, and past 10 people a home connection can't keep up.

25
People in a group

Group conversations are encrypted for each person in them, so they stay small.

12 a day
Relay sign-ins

When two people can't reach each other directly, voice goes through a relay. That costs, so each person gets 12 sign-ins a day; one lasts all day.

1 a second
Spam protection

A burst of 5 messages, then one a second. 10 new servers and 30 friend requests a day per person. It keeps bots and spam out, not you.

What doesn't count

Calls and voice don't go through Hopp Cloud: they go straight between you and your friends, so talking doesn't use anything up. Screen sharing and cameras go the same way.

Want no limits?

Run your own server. It runs on a computer of yours, keeps what you want, and nothing goes through Hopp Cloud. In the app, press + in the server list and choose Run your own server.

Will they change?

Hopp is in beta. If a limit changes, it says so here and on What's new.
