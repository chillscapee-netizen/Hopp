About

What Hopp is

Hopp is an app for talking, playing and hanging out with your friends. Servers, voice channels, calls, cameras and screen sharing, all in one small app.

Why I made Hopp

I wanted a place to hang out with my friends that didn't keep asking for money. In the big chat apps, sharing your screen in good quality, sending bigger files or picking how it all looks sits behind a monthly payment, and more and more of them come with ads, quests and things to buy. Some now even want a photo of your ID or your face.

So I started making my own. One where everything is free, where messages between friends are actually private, where nobody needs your email, phone number or ID, and where the app stays small and quick instead of eating your computer. Where accounts are hard to steal, and where the look is yours to change.

And I wanted it to be fast. A lot of chat apps are a whole web browser in disguise: they take up a big chunk of your memory just sitting there, slow down your games, take ages to start, and voice gets choppy or stuck on "connecting". Hopp is built differently. Its heart is written in Rust, a language made for speed, and it uses the browser engine Windows already has instead of shipping its own. That's why the download is about 5 MB, why it starts in a moment and switches servers at once, and why voice goes straight between you and your friends instead of around the world and back.

Hopp is made by one person, with friends testing it along the way. It's not a company and it doesn't sell anything.

Beta

Hopp is in beta

That means Hopp works and you can use it every day, but it's still young. Honestly:

  • You'll find bugs. New versions come often, sometimes every week, and fix them.
  • Things can change and move as Hopp gets better. Every change is listed on What's new.
  • Hopp Cloud runs on a free plan and can pause for a few hours on a very busy day.
  • It's for Windows only for now.
  • Your account, servers and messages are kept between versions; nothing is wiped when Hopp updates.

Found something broken? In Hopp, open Settings, then App, and press Copy next to Debug info. Send it, with what happened, to the person who invited you or to whoever runs your server.

What Hopp stands for

Hanging out with friends online shouldn't cost money, show you ads or follow you around. So Hopp is free, all of it: screen sharing in full quality, cameras, uploads and every look. There's nothing to unlock.

It's also private where it matters most. Messages and calls between friends are end-to-end encrypted, your Hopp ID is a key that stays on your own computer, and voice goes straight between the people talking whenever it can.

And it's light. Hopp is a small download that starts quickly and switches between servers at once. Updates come when you choose to install them.

How it stays free

Servers made in Hopp run on Hopp Cloud, which uses a free plan. Calls and voice don't go through it at all: they go straight between you and your friends. That's how Hopp can be free with no ads and nothing to pay. A free plan only does so much each day and only stores so much, so Hopp keeps a few limits.

The limits

Every limit Hopp Cloud has, and why it's there, is on its own page: Limits.

Want no limits?

Run your own server. It runs on a computer of yours, keeps what you want, and nothing goes through Hopp Cloud. In the app, press + in the server list and choose Run your own server to see how.

Where Hopp is going

Hopp gets better every week. You can follow along on What's new, and the app tells you when a new version is ready.
